Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Send us a message below.

What is FORE?

FORE is a free library of PDF books. You can read any book online in your browser or download it to keep.

Do I need a subscription to access FORE?

No. Every book is free to read and download, with no subscription and no sign-up.

What are the benefits of paying for a FORE subscription?

There is no paid plan. Everything on FORE is already free, so there is nothing to upgrade to.

Can I upload and share documents on FORE?

Not at the moment. Our team adds the books. If you have a book you’d like to see here, use Contact us to send it our way.

How does FORE handle copyright infringement?

We respect the rights of authors and publishers. When a rights holder tells us a book is on FORE without permission, we review it and remove it if the claim is valid.

How can I report copyright infringement to FORE?

Use Contact us and tell us which book it is, where you found it, and how you own the rights. We will get back to you as soon as we can.

How do I cancel my subscription?

There is nothing to cancel. FORE has no subscriptions, accounts or payments.
